# Field notes: 18 independent checks while launching Agent Referee

On Oct 27, 2026, Agent Referee's own `review-3` (three AI reviewers from three companies) checked the plans, code and deploy checklist for its launch: 18 paid checks, $3.60 in all. These notes record what happened, compare it with published research on AI reviewers, and then correct both after the reviewers checked the write-up itself.

**Start with Part 3.** Parts 1 and 2 are kept exactly as they were checked, so the corrections stay visible.

1. [Part 1: Claude's written log](part-1-log.md). Every check, its votes and what changed, built from the signed receipts. Passed its own check 2 to 1, with errors fixed in Part 3.
2. [Part 2: Published research on AI review panels](part-2-research.md). Deep web research comparing the log with studies from 2023 to 2026. Both halves came back needs_changes.
3. [Part 3: Corrected and combined edition](part-3-corrected.md). Every confirmed error fixed, overstated claims narrowed, and 11 recommendations. Its own check came back needs_changes; all six points were fixed.

4. [Part 4: Ten checks on the ChatGPT build](part-4-chatgpt-build.md). Oct 28: what ten more checks caught (including a privacy bug a test missed), where the briefs were wrong, and what rerunning the same file, with fixed and rotating panels, showed.

Claude wrote all four parts and proposed half of the checks on its own initiative (9 of 18); the owner decided what to apply and ran every paid check.

**Bottom line:** use reviewers to decide what to test, and use tests to decide what ships.
